Let's Talk Doggos on Twitter 🐶

We cleaned up this messy Twitter data to explore what's really poppin' in the world of dog tweets. Here’s what we found:

Insight 1: People Love Big Ratings ❤️

The higher the dog rating (like 13/10 or 14/10), the more likes the tweet gets. Turns out, hyping up doggos gets people to smash that like button.
